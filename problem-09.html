Student Terms & Rules

Welcome to the Student Terms & Rules page. All students are expected to follow these rules carefully.

General Rules

1. Students must maintain proper discipline and respectful behaviour inside the college campus.

2. Students must carry their valid college ID card whenever they are on campus.

Note: Students should follow the instructions given by faculty members and college authorities.

Attendance

3. Students must maintain the minimum required attendance percentage.
Attendance shortage may affect eligibility for examinations.

Important: Regular attendance is compulsory.

Examination

4. Students must carry their examination ID card and required stationery to the examination hall.

5. Any form of cheating or unfair means during an examination is strictly prohibited.
Violation may result in disciplinary action.

Note: Students should reach the examination hall before the scheduled starting time.

Assignment Submission

6. Students must submit assignments before the given deadline.

Late submissions may receive reduced marks or penalties.

Note: Students should keep a backup of their completed assignments.

Discipline

7. Students must maintain discipline and should not disturb classes or other students.

Serious misconduct may lead to disciplinary action.

Respect your teachers, classmates, college staff, and college property.

Important Notice

All students are responsible for reading and following the Student Terms & Rules.

Important Deadline: Submit all assignments on or before the specified due date.
