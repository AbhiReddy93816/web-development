Science Notes

Welcome to the Science Notes page. This page contains important concepts from Chemistry, Physics, and Biology.

Chemistry

Chemistry is the study of matter and its properties.

Some important chemical formulas are:

Water: H2O
Carbon dioxide: CO2
Oxygen: O2
Sulfuric acid: H2SO4
Ammonia: NH3

Atoms are the basic units of matter. A molecule is formed when atoms combine chemically.

Physics

Physics is the study of matter, energy, motion, and forces.

Examples of scientific expressions:

Area = m2
Volume = m3
Speed of light = 3 × 108 m/s

Gravity is the force that attracts objects toward each other.

Newton's second law: F = ma

Biology

Biology is the study of living organisms.

The cell is the basic structural and functional unit of life.

Important biological information:
Humans breathe oxygen (O2).
Plants use carbon dioxide (CO2) during photosynthesis.

Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants prepare their food using sunlight.
